Search syntax

  • !engine or !shortcut — search one engine
  • !category — search a whole category
  • !! — redirect to the first result
  • !!bang — hand the query over to another service
  • :language — force a language (:ja, :japanese)
  • <3 — limit the search to 3 seconds (<850 = 850 ms)
